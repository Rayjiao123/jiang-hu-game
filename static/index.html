身不由己

You didn't choose this inn. Get out of it alive, or don't.

黑沙客栈 · The Black Sand Inn. A sandstorm drives you, a courier's daughter with a secret letter, into a desert inn where three caravans stopped this season and were never seen again. The keeper smiles, the door is barred with an iron lock, and everyone in the hall is caught by something. There are only two ways out: a secret tunnel, or the keeper's key. Find out which you can open before dawn. A wrong word to the wrong person, or a wrong move, and you die. Your game master, Ray, runs the night.

黑沙客栈。一场沙暴，把你，一个带着密信的镖师之女，赶进了大漠里一家客栈：这一季在这里歇脚的三队商旅，再没人见过。掌柜笑盈盈，大门上着铁锁，大堂里每个人都被什么拴着。出路只有两条：一条暗道，或者掌柜的钥匙。在天亮前，弄清你能打开哪一条。对错的人说错一句话，或者走错一步，你就会死。你的说书人兼主持 Ray 会主持这一夜。

玩法 How it plays

  1. 一

    你是青禾 You are Qinghe

    A seventeen-year-old courier's daughter, quick and stubborn, carrying her father's last letter. You didn't pick the story; you pick what to say and who to trust.

    十七岁的镖师之女，机灵又倔强，带着父亲最后一封信。故事不是你选的，你能选的是说什么、信谁。

  2. 二

    用话语闯出去 Talk, carefully

    Everyone in the inn has a secret and a price, and some of them kill for a living. Win their trust, learn the truth of the inn, and find out how to get out. But show the wrong person your letter, or tell them your plan, or push them too far, and the story is over.

    客栈里每个人都有秘密和价码，有些人是靠杀人过活的。赢得他们的信任，弄清客栈的真相，找出离开的办法。可你要是把信亮给错的人，或者说出你的打算，或者把人逼得太紧，故事就结束了。

  3. 三

    Ray 主持这一夜 Ray runs the night

    Your game master narrates, moves the night along, and answers when you're stuck. Every few exchanges an hour passes. If you get out, he tells you the whole story. If you die, he tells you that too.

    说书人 Ray 负责叙述、推进夜色，你卡住的时候他会给你提示。每说几句，一个时辰就过去了。你要是逃出去，他会把整个故事讲给你听；你要是死了，他也会。

客栈里的人 The people in the inn

Six people share this roof tonight. Every one of them is trapped in a different way, and none of them is clean. 今夜同在一个屋檐下的有六个人，每个人被困住的方式都不一样，也没有一个人是干净的。

你的主持人 Your game master

说书人 RayStoryteller and Game Master 说书人 · 主持

Ray is the voice of the story. Like a tabletop game master, he describes the scene, fires the events of the night, and will talk to you at any time: ask what's going on, who someone is, or for a nudge.

Ray 是这个故事的声音。就像桌面游戏的主持人，他描述场景、安排夜里发生的事，随时都可以和你谈：问问眼下的情况、某人是谁，或者要一点提示。

Later chapters will add fighting, which Ray will run the way a game master runs a battle: you describe what you do, he decides what happens. There are three endings in this one: dead, out through the tunnel, or with the keeper's key.

之后的章节会加入战斗，Ray 会像桌面游戏主持人主持战斗那样：你说出你要做什么，他来决定结果。这一章有三个结局：死去、从暗道逃出，或夺下掌柜的钥匙。
