你已身在江湖

You're already in the jianghu. Come in and have a look.

Make your own swordsman in one sentence, walk into Mount Hua in the week of its great sword gathering, and talk to the people there: a champion in hiding, a proud young swordswoman, an innkeeper who knows too much. Every one is an AI with secrets of their own, and every one reacts to who you are.

一句话创建你的侠客，在华山剑会前夕走上山去，和山上的人交谈：隐匿的魁首、骄傲的少女剑客、知道得太多的客栈掌柜。每个人都是有自己秘密的 AI，并且会根据你是谁做出不同的反应。

华山上的人 Who is on Mount Hua right now

Seven days until the Summit Sword Gathering. The last champion has not been seen for a month. Everyone you meet wants something from you, and is hiding something. 七日后绝顶剑会开幕，上届魁首已一月不见。你遇到的每个人都想从你这里得到点什么，也都在隐瞒点什么。

江湖正在展开 The jianghu is opening up

We are starting with one iconic place, and filling it with real characters, before opening the rest. Tap Mount Hua to begin. The padlocked places open later, one by one.

我们先从一个标志性的地方开始，把它填满真正的人物，再陆续开放其他地方。点「华山」即可开始，带锁的地方之后逐一开放。
