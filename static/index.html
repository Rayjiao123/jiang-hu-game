谁杀了你的爱人

Someone on Mount Hua killed the one you love. Find out who.

Make your hero in one sentence, then climb Mount Hua in the week of its great sword gathering. Question the people there: a proud young swordswoman, an innkeeper who counts footsteps, a healer bound by an oath, a stranger with a saber. Every one is an AI with secrets, and every one reacts to who you are. Earn their trust, gather the clues, and name the killer.

一句话创建你的侠客，在华山剑会前夕走上山去，向山上的人问话：骄傲的少女剑客、数得清脚步声的客栈掌柜、受誓言所缚的医女、带着刀的陌生人。每个人都是有秘密的 AI，并且会根据你是谁做出不同的反应。赢得信任，收集线索，指认凶手。

玩法 How it plays

  1. 一

    创建你的侠客 Make your hero

    Say one sentence, or roll the dice. You get a path, a manner, a signature move, a quirk, and the person you lost. Change any of them before you go in.

    说一句话，或掷骰子。你会得到道、性情、绝技、习癖，以及你失去的爱人。进入江湖前，每一项都能修改。

  2. 二

    向人问话 Question them as you

    Everyone on the mountain knows who you are, and reacts to your path and your manner. Win their trust and they give up what they know. Push too hard and they close up.

    山上的人都知道你是谁，并会因你的道与性情而不同。赢得信任，他们才肯说出所知；逼得太紧，他们就闭口不言。

  3. 三
    证手地人因

    指认凶手 Name the killer

    Collect clues in your case file. When you have proof of three different kinds (how, where, who, why, who it could not be), accuse someone. You get three chances.

    把线索收进案卷。凑齐三种不同的证据（手法、地点、在场者、动机、排除嫌疑）后，指认凶手。你有三次机会。

山上的嫌疑人 The people on the mountain

Three days ago a guest was found dead below the Sword Cliff. The Pavilion says it was a fall, and buried them before noon. Seven days until the Sword Gathering. Everyone here knows something, and nobody tells it for free. 三天前，有位客人被发现死在剑崖下。云剑阁说是失足，中午前就下了葬。七日后剑会开幕。这里每个人都知道些什么，却没人会白白告诉你。
