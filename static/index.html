身不由己

You didn't choose this inn. Now find your way out of it.

黑沙客栈 · The Black Sand Inn. A sandstorm has trapped you, a courier's daughter with a secret letter, in an inn in the desert that is not what it seems. The keeper smiles, the door is barred, and every person in the hall is caught by something too. Talk to them, learn what they want, win their trust, and get out before dawn. Your game master, Ray, runs the night.

黑沙客栈。一场沙暴，把你，一个带着密信的镖师之女，困在了大漠里一家并不简单的客栈。掌柜笑盈盈，大门闩着，大堂里的每个人也都身不由己。和他们交谈，弄清他们要什么，赢得信任，在天亮前逃出去。你的说书人兼主持 Ray，会主持这一夜。

玩法 How it plays

  1. 一

    你是青禾 You are Qinghe

    A seventeen-year-old courier's daughter, quick and stubborn, carrying her father's last letter. You didn't pick the story; you pick what to say and who to trust.

    十七岁的镖师之女，机灵又倔强，带着父亲最后一封信。故事不是你选的，你能选的是说什么、信谁。

  2. 二

    用话语闯出去 Talk your way out

    Everyone in the inn has a secret and a price. Be kind, be clever, be honest or be careful: they remember how you treat them. Find a way out and a way past the guards.

    客栈里每个人都有秘密，也都有价码。善意、聪明、坦诚或谨慎：他们记得你怎样对待他们。找一条出路，再找一个避开守卫的办法。

  3. 三

    Ray 主持这一夜 Ray runs the night

    Your game master narrates, moves the night along, and answers when you're stuck. Every few exchanges an hour passes. At dawn, the story is over.

    说书人 Ray 负责叙述、推进夜色，你卡住的时候他会给你提示。每说几句，一个时辰就过去了。天一亮，故事就结束。

客栈里的人 The people in the inn

Six people share this roof tonight. Every one of them is trapped in a different way. 今夜同在一个屋檐下的有六个人，每个人被困住的方式都不一样。

你的主持人 Your game master

说书人 RayStoryteller and Game Master 说书人 · 主持

Ray is the voice of the story. Like a tabletop game master, he describes the scene, fires the events of the night, and will talk to you at any time: ask what's going on, who someone is, or for a nudge.

Ray 是这个故事的声音。就像桌面游戏的主持人，他描述场景、安排夜里发生的事，随时都可以和你谈：问问眼下的情况、某人是谁，或者要一点提示。

Later chapters will add fighting, which Ray will run the way a game master runs a battle: you describe what you do, he decides what happens.

之后的章节会加入战斗，Ray 会像桌面游戏主持人主持战斗那样：你说出你要做什么，他来决定结果。
